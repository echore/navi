<p>My curiosity about AI has gone through three stages.</p>
<p>In the first stage, I was amazed by how powerful agent tools were, and I started using agents to build my own little projects. Along the way, though, I felt that everything I made was AI slop, and I wasn't happy with it.</p>
<p>That led me to the second stage. I watched a lot of YouTube videos on how to work better with agents, and I followed all kinds of AI trends. It wasn't only agents. Whatever else came up, I'd try it. Looking back, this stage was more restless. You know the thing is powerful, but you can't tell which parts are good and which are bad. I'd watch one demo and think, "Oh, that's cool!" Every day there was something new on X. It looked impressive, like it could change the world, but it never did.</p>
<p>Once I had more or less figured out how to work with agents, I slowly moved into the third stage, which is where I am now. AI is a tool, so I wanted to know what was actually inside it. For me it was a black box, and I wanted to open it up and see how it works and how it's built. That's when I got interested in AI itself and the theory behind it. I wanted to know where it came from, its history, and how it grew into what it is today.</p>
<p>In the end, I chose to watch some high-quality open courses. This brought me back to the meta-skill we started with: how to learn. I used the same method as always. I talked through my needs and ideas with AI, and together we built a learning tree. One thing I cared about a lot was quality: I wanted high-quality courses, ideally free ones. I also believe many open courses from the world's top universities really are free. If you want to learn, the internet has a huge amount of free, high-quality videos, books, and other resources. What matters is whether you want to learn, and your own drive.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The first course I watched was <a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXV9Vh2jYcjbnv67sXNDJiO8MWLA3ZJKR">MIT 6.S087: Foundation Models &amp; Generative AI (2024)</a>.</p>
<p>I'd give it somewhere between 3.5 and 4 stars. It's fairly friendly to people without a related background, and you really don't need any technical training to understand it. But if you know nothing at all about AI, it may still be a little hard.</p>
<p>It covers fairly basic questions. How did AI get to where it is today? What are supervised learning and reinforcement learning? Why did foundation models appear? What are the basic principles behind ChatGPT and image generation?</p>
<p>The instructor explains things in plain language, with lots of examples and analogies, and I found the structure and flow of the whole course quite clear. Overall, I'd recommend it.</p>
<p>Its weakness is also obvious: it's a 2024 course. The principles it teaches haven't changed, which is why I think it's still worth watching. But the slides themselves have a strong 2024 feel. Their generated images are nowhere near what image generation can do in 2026.</p>
<p>Still, that's a small flaw. It doesn't hurt the big picture, so I think it's fine. Fundamentals like these won't change much in the next few years, so it doesn't matter much which course you pick. If you're curious about the principles and mechanisms behind AI, watch it. Just know that it isn't a course on how to use AI well.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The second course I watched was <a href="https://introtodeeplearning.com/">MIT 6.S191 Introduction to Deep Learning</a>.</p>
<p>It has run as a cohort for many years, the content is updated every year, and all the videos, slides, and labs are public. My personal, slightly biased rating is about 4.5 stars.</p>
<p>I think everything about it, including the overall structure, is very good. It's a bit more advanced than the first course. I don't have a technical background or any formal training, and I taught myself everything I know. Even so, if you're interested enough, you can still understand it and keep up.</p>
<p>This course goes one step further and gets into the inside of deep learning. How does a neural network actually learn? How are a model's parameters updated? Why can a Transformer handle language? How does AI generate images and learn to make decisions? The course covers deep learning basics, sequence models, computer vision, generative models, reinforcement learning, and more.</p>
<p>Honestly, I couldn't follow every part of this course. Sometimes I just didn't get it, and I was often like, "Wait, what does this mean?" When that happened, I'd take a screenshot, throw it at ChatGPT along with the YouTube transcript, and ask it to explain. ChatGPT went into more detail, and its explanations were easier to understand. The course also has some math formulas, and that's where AI helped most. I used to have zero interest in learning math formulas. But when AI walked me through them, it broke each one down in detail and then put the pieces back together. I realized it wasn't that hard after all. I just never had a guide this good before.</p>
<p>Overall, the structure is well designed. You can also do their labs for hands-on practice. The end of the course is a bit different every year: they invite different speakers to talk about real-world practice and cases from industry or academia. I haven't listened to those myself, haha. But if you're interested, I'd still recommend this course.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Summary</strong></p>
<p>I studied these because I was curious about how AI itself works. GPT custom-built my course roadmap, and I don't watch every lecture. Different people have different needs, and you can ask AI to build a personalized plan for you. Both courses are high quality in content. Whether they're right for you depends on your own goals and needs.</p>

<p>我对 AI 的好奇经历了三个阶段。</p>
<p>第一个阶段是惊叹于 Agent 工具的强大，并开始用 Agent 去做自己的一些小玩意。但在这个过程中，我觉得自己做的都是一些 AI slop，对此并不满意。</p>
<p>于是进入了第二阶段：去 YouTube 上大量地看如何更好地和 Agent 协作，包括去看各种 AI 的趋势。不仅仅有 Agent，可能还有其他的，我都会去试一试。我觉得这个阶段其实是更浮躁的，因为你知道这个东西很强大，但你其实并不能分辨出哪些是好的、哪些是坏的。我只是看了一个 demo，只觉得“哦，很酷！”每天都会发现，这个在 X 上又有什么新东西了。这看起来很厉害，好像可以改变世界那种，但其实没有的。</p>
<p>后面我如何和 Agent 去合作这个学得差不多了以后，我就渐渐到了第三个阶段，也就是现在的阶段。它是一个工具，那我想知道这个工具里面到底是什么。它对于我来说是一个黑盒的状态，我就想看看这个盒子里面是什么，它是怎么工作、怎么构造的。然后我就开始对 AI 本身或者它背后的理论感兴趣。我就想知道它的来时路、它的历史，它到底怎么发展到现在这样。</p>
<p>最后我选择去看一些比较高质量的公开课。这又回到了我们最初的元能力，就是如何学习。我还是那套方法：和 AI 讨论我的需求、我的想法，最后做出来一个 learning tree 知识树。并且我非常强调的是，我想要高质量的公开课，它最好也是免费的。我也相信，像世界名校的公开课，其实很多真的就是免费的。只要想学的话，互联网有大量免费的、非常高质量的视频、书籍或者任何资源，其实只看自己想不想学，自己的自驱力。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>我看的第一个公开课就是这个：<a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXV9Vh2jYcjbnv67sXNDJiO8MWLA3ZJKR">MIT 6.S087: Foundation Models &amp; Generative AI (2024)</a>。</p>
<p>我会给它打 3.5 星到 4 星之间吧。我觉得它对于一个没有相关背景的人还是比较友好的，确实不需要任何技术背景，也能够理解。但如果你对 AI 完全不了解的话，它可能还是有一点点难度。</p>
<p>它涉及到的还是一些更基础的问题。比如，AI 是怎么发展到现在的？什么是监督学习、强化学习？为什么会出现 Foundation Models？ChatGPT 和图像生成背后的基本原理是什么？</p>
<p>这个老师讲得还是比较通俗易懂的，会有大量的例子、类比去讲述，包括整个的结构设计脉络，我觉得还是比较清晰的。总体还是比较推荐这个课。</p>
<p>它的缺点也比较明显，就是这是 2024 年的课程。它讲的一些原理部分是没有变过的，所以我觉得这是依然值得看的理由。但它这个课件本身，可以看到，是有浓浓的 2024 年的风格。它的生图不像 2026 年已经可以做到非常好的地步。</p>
<p>但我觉得这只能说是美中不足，不能说是影响大局，所以我觉得还好。像这种非常基础的，我觉得这几年是不会变的，所以看什么都可以。如果你对 AI 本身背后的一些原理和方法机制感兴趣，就可以看，但它不是一个如何用好 AI 的课程。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>第二个我看的是 <a href="https://introtodeeplearning.com/">MIT 6.S191 Introduction to Deep Learning</a>。</p>
<p>它是已经开了很多年的 Cohort 了，每年都会更新课程内容，而且所有的视频、Slides 和 Labs 都会公开。我私心会给他打到 4.5 星左右。</p>
<p>我觉得它整体的结构设计等等，所有都是非常好的。它比第一个要稍微进阶一点，作为我是没有技术基础背景的，没有受过系统的训练，所有东西都是自学的。只要足够感兴趣，即使它在基础的过程上还是有一点点进阶的，但还是可以理解的，还是可以跟上的。</p>
<p>这门课会更进一步，真正进入深度学习的内部。比如，神经网络到底是怎么学习的？模型的参数是如何更新的？为什么 Transformer 能处理语言？AI 又是如何生成图像、学习决策的？课程涵盖深度学习基础、序列模型、计算机视觉、生成模型、强化学习等内容。</p>
<p>我在这个课程上，其实并不是每一个片段都能跟上。有的时候我也不理解，经常 be like 这什么意思呢？我就直接会截图，并且会把 YouTube scripts 扔给 ChatGPT，让它给我解释。这样的话，ChatGPT 其实会解释得更加详细，也会更好理解。包括这里面还是会有一些数学公式的，这个时候用 AI 是非常好的。因为我以前是根本不想学这些数学公式的，但我发现 AI 跟我讲了之后，它是真的把每一个都拆得非常详细，然后再组合在一起。我就会发现，其实它并没有那么难，其实它不难，只是曾经没有这样好的一个指导的资源。</p>
<p>总的来说整个的结构设计挺好的。同时也可以做它们的 lab，它们有一些实践。最后他们每年课程最后是有些不一样的，会请不同的 speakers 去讲一些业界或者学术界的实践、真实世界的案例。虽然我没有听吼，但如果你感兴趣的话，我还是比较推荐这个课程的。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>总结</strong></p>
<p>我去学这些，是基于对 AI 原理本身的好奇，GPT 为我定制的课程路线图，我并不是每节课都会看；不同的人有不同的需求，可以让 AI 为之个性化定制，以上两个课程从内容本身都是较为优质的，只是看是否符合个人目标和需求。</p>
